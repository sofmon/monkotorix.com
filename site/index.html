Our Story

Monkotorix was born from the effort of technical parents to support their neurodivergent kids as best they can — with the tools they know and master: technology.

We made these games for our own children first, and play-tested them at the kitchen table. Now we’re sharing them, free, in the hope that other families enjoy them as much as ours do.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Platform

A playful space for every kind of mind

Monkotorix is a collection of games and playful activities for children: movement challenges, puzzles, numbers, letters and words, memory games and more — on the screen and off it.

We design every game with different children and different ways of playing in mind. Activities come in different levels and can be played at each child’s own pace, on their own or side by side with a grown-up.

The Games

Two games, now in open beta

Free to play in the browser on tablets, phones and computers. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking — just open a game and play together.

NeuroCrew

Beta

Movement & puzzle games · Ages 4–8

Short, playful games for hands, eyes and the whole body. A friendly buddy shows every move as children trace, pop, catch, copy and balance their way along a magic path through five worlds — with a breathing break always one tap away.

  • 21 games in six groups — from tracing and catching to patterns and little sudokus
  • Separate levels for ages 4–5 and 6–8
  • Calm mode, a slower speed and read-aloud instructions
  • A “Try it for real” idea to play away from the screen after every level
  • In English and Dutch
Play NeuroCrew
Ages 4–8 English · Nederlands Phone, tablet & computer

Lezen met Olli

Beta

Dutch letters & words game · Ages 5–8

A cheerful Dutch game full of letters, sounds and words, made to be calm, clear and easy on the eyes. Olli the owl walks children along a magic path through four worlds of short games — find the letter, pop the right balloon, spot the rhyme, build the word — and reads the instructions aloud.

  • 11 letter and word games, plus a movement break
  • Four worlds, three levels and twelve badges to earn
  • No timers or lives — a wrong answer just means try again
  • Bold text with wide letter spacing
  • Sound groups like “aa”, “oe” and “ui” always stay together
  • Olli speaks with your device’s Dutch voice — most phones, tablets and computers have one. Without one, the game still works, just without Olli’s voice.

What “beta” means

Both games are ready to play — and still growing. You may spot rough edges, and things will change as we hear from families. Progress is saved only in the browser on your device, so it won’t follow you to another device or browser.

Designed for Different Minds

Games that fit the child

Every child has their own mix of interests, energy and ways of playing. That’s why our games come with different levels, settings a grown-up can adjust, and room to play at each child’s own pace.

“Rather than expecting children to fit the game, we want the game to fit the child.”

  • Unhurried by design

    Short rounds and no lives to lose. Breaks are built in, and NeuroCrew slows down when a child needs more time.

  • Hear every instruction

    Instructions can be heard and replayed. Olli reads each one aloud, and in NeuroCrew a tap on “Hear it” does the same — or a grown-up can switch on read-aloud for every level.

  • Mistakes are part of play

    No buzzers and nothing to lose. A wrong try brings a gentle hint and another go.

  • Small wins, celebrated

    Stars, stickers and badges to collect along the way, with a sticker at the end of every round.

  • Predictable and clear

    The same clear steps every round, and a magic path that shows the whole plan before play begins.

  • Gentle on the senses

    Motion calms down when a device asks for less, sound and voice can be switched off, and both games come in light and dark.

All of this is already part of both beta games.
